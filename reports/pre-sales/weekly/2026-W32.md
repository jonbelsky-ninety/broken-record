# Weekly Feedback Intelligence — Pre-sales — 2026-W32 (2026-06-29 → 2026-08-03 UTC)

**The week in one line:** 78 pre-sales calls. These prospects are overwhelmingly early EOS adopters hunting for one system to replace scattered spreadsheets, docs, and legacy tools and run tighter meetings, with pricing/licensing, integrations, and migration off competitors the places they most stall.

## Themes this week
- Prospects want to consolidate spreadsheets, Google docs, and slides into a single operating home.
- Many are brand-new to EOS and coach- or implementer-led, wanting guided setup so they start right.
- Per-user pricing, license tiers, and role/permission clarity surface repeatedly as adoption concerns.
- Integration coverage across Outlook, CRM, spreadsheets, and project tools recurs as a gating question.
- Several are migrating from or comparing against competitors, frustrated by weak reporting or drift from the EOS framework.

### About this report
78 pre-sales Gong calls this window — all **extracted** (sales-convened conversations), so volume reflects the sales calendar, not demand. This cohort is prospects and not-yet-resolved companies, so it has **no Operating Cadence Score (OCS)** — nothing here corroborates product behaviour; it is prospect *say* on its own terms. Company resolved for 2 of 78 (the rest show only a contact). Contact and any company come from our systems in code — the model supplies only wants / blocker / enthusiasm. **Purity caveat:** an `unknown` company means no resolved customer record; a former or existing customer whose company did not resolve would also land here, so treat a call mentioning existing licenses or prior usage with care.

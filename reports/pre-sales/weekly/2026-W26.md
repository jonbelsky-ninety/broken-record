### About this report
20 pre-sales Gong calls this window — all **extracted** (sales-convened conversations), so volume reflects the sales calendar, not demand. This cohort is prospects and not-yet-resolved companies, so it has **no Operating Cadence Score (OCS)** — nothing here corroborates product behaviour; it is prospect *say* on its own terms.

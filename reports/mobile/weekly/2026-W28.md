### About this report
15 pieces of feedback this window. **14 came from sales or support calls we convened (Gong, Intercom); 1 was volunteered unprompted in-app.** Unprompted feedback is weighted more heavily, because call volume reflects our sales calendar, not demand. **To follow up** ranks the sharpest feedback first and gives extra weight to paying accounts **at risk of churn**, so a struggling or ghosting account's complaint outranks a healthy one's. 5 of the 15 come from paying accounts with an **Operating Cadence Score (OCS)** — Ninety's read on how consistently a company runs the core operating workflow (L10s, Scorecard, Rocks, To-Dos, Issues); the Segment column is that account's OCS archetype (Power User, Solid Operator, Struggler, Ghost, or Newcomer). Trials and prospects have no cadence score yet. Company, contact, and segment come from our systems in code — never the model, which only summarizes each note and drafts the suggested action.
